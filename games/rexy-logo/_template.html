the Rexy maze, traced end to end

Steady hands. Follow the maze, don't touch the walls.

  • Take control by moving your cursor onto the marker.
  • The marker then follows your hand exactly — you are the cursor.
  • Drift near a wall and the marker glows red as a warning.
  • Clip a wall or a cone and the penalty clock runs.
  • Go right through something and you're out — back to the start.
  • The maze has dead ends. Take one and you only lose time.
  • Esc pauses. F toggles full screen.
